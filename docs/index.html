Key Capabilities

Engineered for Sovereign Performance

Direct MTProto TLS

Direct socket streams to official Telegram CDN datacenters (149.154.167.50:443). No third-party relays, no intermediary cloud servers, and no rate limit taxes.

Dedicated Storage Vault

TGCloud automatically discovers or provisions a private channel (TGCloud Storage Vault). Your files stay organized and completely separate from personal chats.

Resumable 512 KB Transfers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Do I need to create or configure API keys to use TGCloud? +
No! Official Telegram client credentials are baked directly into the standalone application. You simply launch TGCloud, enter your standard phone number, confirm the 5-digit verification code sent to your Telegram app, and your cloud drive is ready.
Does TGCloud clutter my personal "Saved Messages" chat? +
No. TGCloud automatically provisions a dedicated, private channel titled TGCloud Storage Vault accessible only by your account. All folders, files, and backups are stored there, keeping your personal chats clean.
Are my Telegram 2FA cloud passwords stored on disk? +
Never. Telegram Two-Step Verification passwords are used ephemerally in-memory strictly during the SRP authentication challenge and are immediately zeroized. They are never written to disk or sent to any third party.
What is the maximum file size supported? +
Telegram supports up to 2 GB per individual file for standard accounts, and up to 4 GB per file for Telegram Premium users. There is no limit on the total number of files or total capacity.
Can I share files to TGCloud directly from my Android Gallery? +
Yes! TGCloud registers native Android SEND and SEND_MULTIPLE intent filters. Simply tap Share on any photo, video, or document in your Gallery and select TGCloud to queue background uploads.
